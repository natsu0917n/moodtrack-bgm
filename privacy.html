Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains how Moodtrack BGM uploader (the "App") handles data. The App is a private tool used only by the operator of the YouTube channel Moodtrack BGM to upload the channel's own videos. It is not offered to other users.

Use of YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the App accesses

  • Channel information (channel name and ID), only to confirm that videos are uploaded to the correct channel.
  • Upload permission, to upload videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails that the channel operator prepared.

The App does not read comments, viewer data, analytics, or any data about other YouTube users.

How data is stored and shared

  • The OAuth access token is stored only on the operator's own computer and is never sent anywhere except Google's servers.
  • The App does not collect, sell, or share any personal data with third parties.
  • The App does not use cookies or tracking.

Revoking access

Access granted to the App can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. Deleting the locally stored token also stops the App from accessing the account.
